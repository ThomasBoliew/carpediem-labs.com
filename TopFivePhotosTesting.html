Thank you for helping test Top Five Photos before its public release.

Before you start

Closed testing on Google Play usually requires three steps. Use the same Google account for each step: the Google Group, the testing opt-in page, and the Play Store install.

1. Join the tester group

Join the Google Group first. This is what gives your Google account permission to access the closed test.

Join the tester group

2. Opt in to the test

After joining the group, open the Google Play testing link and choose to become a tester.

Opt in to the test

3. Install from Google Play

Once you are opted in, install Top Five Photos from Google Play on an Android device signed in with the same Google account. If the listing is not available yet, wait a little while and try again.

Install on Android

4. Send feedback

If something is confusing, broken, slow, ugly, or just feels wrong, please send a note. Screenshots and device model names are helpful.

Email feedback

Troubleshooting

  • If Google Play says the app is not available, make sure you joined the tester Google Group with the same account you use in the Play Store.
  • If you just joined the group or the test was just published, Google Play may need time before the app is available.
  • If you use more than one Google account on your device, switch Play Store to the account that joined the test.
