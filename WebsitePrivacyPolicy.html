Carpe Diem Labs Website Privacy Notice

Effective Date: September 24, 2026

Carpe Diem Labs builds apps that do not collect personal information or track their users. This notice applies only to the Carpe Diem Labs website. The privacy policy for each app is available separately on that app’s page.

  1. Limited Website Information
    When you visit this website, limited technical information may be recorded automatically by the services that host and deliver it. This may include your IP address, browser and device type, the page requested, the date and time of the visit, and the website or campaign link that referred you here.
  2. How This Information May Be Used
    Carpe Diem Labs may use limited, generally aggregated website information to understand whether pages are working, measure general traffic, compare the usefulness of advertising campaigns, and learn whether visitors follow links to an app store.
  3. What We Do Not Do
    Carpe Diem Labs does not sell website visitor information. We do not use information from this website to build personal profiles, and we do not combine website activity with photos, galleries, or other information stored inside our apps.
  4. Links to Other Services
    This website links to services such as the Apple App Store, Google Play, Facebook, and email. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and may collect information when you visit them.
  5. Changes to This Notice
    This notice may be updated if the website or the way its traffic is measured changes. The current version will be posted here with a revised effective date.
  6. Contact
    Questions about this notice can be sent to carpediemlabs@gmail.com.
