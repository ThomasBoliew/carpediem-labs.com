Privacy Policy for Top Five Photos

Effective Date: July 25, 2026

Top Five Photos is operated by Carpe Diem Labs. This policy explains how the app handles privacy. The short version is simple: Top Five Photos does not collect personal information.

  1. Information Collection and Use
    Top Five Photos does not collect, request, process, sell, or share personal information. The app does not require an account, login, name, email address, phone number, or profile.
  2. Photos and Galleries
    Top Five Photos is a gallery app that lets users select photos for personal galleries, including secure and hidden gallery areas. Photos, gallery selections, and secure-gallery contents remain on the user’s device. Carpe Diem Labs does not receive, view, store, or transmit those photos or gallery selections.
  3. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking
    Top Five Photos does not use analytics, advertising trackers, behavioral tracking, or third-party data collection services.
  4. Data Storage
    Any app settings or gallery choices are stored locally on the user’s device for the app to function. Carpe Diem Labs does not operate a server for Top Five Photos and does not back up or remotely access user data.
  5. Children’s Privacy
    Top Five Photos does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
  6. Security
    Because Top Five Photos does not collect or transmit personal information to Carpe Diem Labs, there is no personal information stored by Carpe Diem Labs that could be exposed through our systems.
  7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
    Carpe Diem Labs may update this Privacy Policy when needed. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
  8. Contact Information
    If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Top Five Photos, contact Carpe Diem Labs at:
    Email: carpediemlabs@gmail.com

By using Top Five Photos, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
