Simple tools for everyday life

Carpe Diem Labs creates thoughtful apps that make everyday tasks a little easier. Our goal is simple: remove needless friction so you can spend more time and attention on what matters to you.

The Carpe Diem promise

No subscriptions. Ever.

When you pay for one of our apps, you own it. If you purchase an upgrade, you own that upgrade forever, too. We believe paid apps should be maintained and available for as long as smartphones and tablets exist—not rented back to you one month or year at a time.
